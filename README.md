# Seasonal-Agriculture-Performance-Analysis-
Seasonal Agriculture Analysis using Python explores crop yield, resource usage, and profitability across Kharif, Rabi, and Summer. Through data cleaning, visualization, and statistical tests, it uncovers patterns, relationships, and anomalies to support evidence‑based agricultural planning.
# 🌾 Agricultural Yield & Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores **crop yield and profitability across different seasons and districts in India** using Python, Pandas, Seaborn, and Matplotlib.  
The analysis focuses on how environmental factors such as rainfall, temperature, humidity, and soil conditions influence agricultural productivity.  
By combining **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** with visual storytelling, the project highlights key insights that can guide farmers, policymakers, and researchers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Compare **yield distributions** across seasons (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid).
- Analyze **district‑wise average yield and profit**.
- Study the impact of **rainfall and temperature** on yield.
- Visualize **state‑wise seasonal yield patterns**.
- Identify correlations between **environmental factors and yield**.
- Provide actionable insights for **agricultural planning and decision‑making**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python** (Data analysis & visualization)
- **Pandas** (Data manipulation)
- **NumPy** (Numerical operations)
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Visualizations)
- **Jupyter Notebook** (Interactive analysis)

---

## 📊 Key Visualizations
- **Box Plot:** Crop yield distribution across seasons.  
- **Bar Chart:** District‑wise average yield and profit comparison.  
- **Scatter Plots:**  
  - Rainfall vs Yield by season.  
  - Temperature vs Yield by season.  
- **Line Graph:** Yield patterns across states and seasons.  
- **Heatmap:** Correlation between environmental factors and yield.  
- **Bar Plot:** Average profit across seasons.

---

## 🔎 Insights
- **Kharif** yields are highly dependent on rainfall, showing wide variability.  
- **Rabi** yields are more stable due to irrigation, though profits are moderate.  
- **Zaid** season struggles with low yields and profits, but outliers suggest potential with innovative practices.  
- Districts like **Warangal** show high profitability despite moderate yields, while **Rajkot** leads in yield but not in profit.  
- **Rainfall and soil moisture** are the strongest drivers of yield, while extreme temperature negatively impacts productivity.

- Dataset may not capture **micro‑level variations** (village/farm level).  
- Profit calculations assume average market conditions, not accounting for **price fluctuations**.  
- Environmental data is aggregated and may miss **extreme local events**.  
- Analysis is exploratory; predictive accuracy requires **advanced modeling**.
